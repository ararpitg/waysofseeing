Paths Through the Archive

Five curated paths organize the collection. The first three carry forward the archive’s original structure; the last two name the questions that structure has always been asking. Choose a path to filter the catalog below.

Indigeneity & Culture Cultural practice and questions of representation, through works made by community-curated production processes. Open path → Indigeneity & Nature Land, ecology, food, and livelihood, and the human–nature relationship as Indigenous life-worlds imagine it. Open path → Indigeneity & Youth Work created by young people, or in which young people are the central stakeholders. Open path → Archives & Memory Community archives, counter-archives, and platforms that keep records outside the state and the museum. Open path → Method & Governance Protocols, principles, and tools for holding Indigenous knowledge responsibly in digital form. Open path →

All Records

Every record follows the same metadata model (described under First Principles). Filter by type, path, or region, search across all fields, and open any record for its full metadata and a ready-to-copy citation. Each record has a permanent identifier — add it to this page’s address (for example, #WOS-001) to link straight to it.
